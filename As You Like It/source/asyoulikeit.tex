Based on extant records and intertextual evidence, William Shakespeare (1564--1616) most likely wrote \textit{As You Like It}  between 1598 and 1600. It was first published by William Jaggard in London, in 1623, as part of \textit{Mr. William Shakespeare's Comedies, Histories, \& Tragedies; Published according to the True and Original Copies}: the <First Folio>.

Illustrations by Hugh Thomson (1860--1920) are from a 1902 edition published by Hodder \& Stoughton in London (UK). Grataeful acknowledgement is made to the Internet Archive, which in 2008 digitised a copy of this edition belonging to the University of California library system.
