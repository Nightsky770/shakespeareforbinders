\enter{Touchstone, Audrey}


\speech[Touchstone ]{Tomorrow is the joyful day, Audrey. Tomorrow will we be married.}

\speech[Audrey]{I do desire it with all my heart, and I hope it is no dishonest desire to desire to be a woman of the world.

\speech[Touchstone]{By my troth, well met. Come, sit, sit, and a song.}
