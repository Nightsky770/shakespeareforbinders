The first documented public performance of \textit{Twelfth Night} was on 2 February 1602. Twelfth Night--the night before Epiphany (6 January), or the twelfth night after Christmas--was, in Elizabethan times, a festival of licensed disorder presided over by a <Lord of Misrule>, so William Shakespeare's (1564--1616) merry comedy of mistaken identities would have been suitably <topsy-turvy> entertainment. \textit{Twelfth Night} was first published by William Jaggard in London, in 1623, as part of \textit{Mr. William Shakespeare's Comedies, Histories, \& Tragedies; Published according to the True and Original Copies}: the <First Folio>.

Illustrations by British artist William Heath Robinson (1872--1944) are from a 1913 edition published by Hodder \& Stoughton in London (UK).
