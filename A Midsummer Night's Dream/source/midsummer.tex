\textit{A Midsummer Night's Dream} was first performed in 1595 or 1596, after William Shakespeare (1564--1616) had written \textit{Romeo and Juliet} and probably just before \textit{The Merchant of Venice.} It was first published in 1600 (the <first quarto> edition) by William Jaggard, in London (UK).

Illustrations by British artist Arthur Rackham (1867--1939) are from a 1908 edition published by Heinemann in London (UK) and Doubleday in New York (US). Grateful acknowledgement is made to the Internet Archive, which in 2007 digitised a copy of this edition belonging to the New York Public Library. Additional illustrations are taken from various other Rackham works.
\end{minipage}
